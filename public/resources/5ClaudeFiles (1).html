// Overview
How the system works

One file loads every single session. It sets the rules and tells Claude where to find the rest. The other four only get pulled in when they're actually needed — saving tokens and keeping sessions fast.

Together, they give Claude a complete picture of you — without loading everything at once.

01 / CLAUDE.md
The brain
// The master instruction file
The Master File
CLAUDE
.md
The operating system for every session

This is the first file Claude reads. It sets the rules for how Claude behaves — and tells it exactly where to find everything else. Think of it as the command center, not the encyclopedia.

  • // Keep it short. It doesn't hold your whole life story — it points to where your story lives.
  • // Write your operating rules here. How you like outputs formatted. What to always do. What to never do.
  • // Include explicit "when to read" rules for each file — not just a list of what they contain. "Read voice.md before writing any copy" is an instruction. A table is a reference. Claude follows instructions.
  • // Claude re-reads this every session. Every rule you write here is applied automatically — no reminders required.

02 / soul.md
Who you are
// Your story, your beliefs, your core
Your Origin
soul
.md
The human behind the work

This is not your resume. This is you. Your origin story, your beliefs, the moments that changed your direction. Claude needs to know the human before it can sound like one.

  • // Write your origin story. Where you started, what you walked away from, what changed. Specific moments land harder than summary statements.
  • // Write your actual beliefs — not the safe ones. The opinions you hold about your industry, your craft. These make content feel like you.
  • // soul.md is NOT where your offer or audience lives. This file is about the operator. Keep it personal.
  • // Claude draws on this when it writes in your voice, tells your story, or needs to make a judgment call that reflects your values.
// Prompt — Claude interviews you to generate this file
03 / design.md
How you look
// Colors, fonts, and visual rules
Visual Identity
design
.md
The file that makes Claude output look like you

Every color. Every font. Every visual rule. When Claude generates a carousel, an infographic, or any piece of design output, it checks here first. Without this file, everything comes out generic.

  • // List every brand color with its hex code and when to use it. Don't describe colors in words — give Claude the exact codes.
  • // Name your fonts: display font, body font, any specialty fonts. Include when each one gets used.
  • // Describe your visual aesthetic in 2-3 adjectives, then explain what that means in practice. "Minimal" means different things to different people.
  • // Include the things you never want to see. No gradients. No stock photos. No rounded corners. The no-list is just as important as the yes-list.
// Prompt — drop in a screenshot, then paste this
04 / voice.md
How you talk
// Tone, phrasing, and word rules
Communication Rules
voice
.md
Your words. Your tone. Your rules.

This is where Claude learns how you actually talk. Not a generic brand voice doc — your specific phrases, your specific rules, the words you never say and the patterns you always use.

  • // List the words you never use. These are often more useful than what you do use. "Solopreneur" — never. "Leverage" as a verb — never. Be specific.
  • // The prompt works from memory and conversation history — no samples required. Bonus: paste in a post, caption, or email and it extracts the rules directly from your actual language.
  • // Break down your voice by format. How you write DMs is different from how you write posts. Both belong here.
  • // Write your communication philosophy in one or two sentences. Why do you talk the way you do? The reason behind a rule helps Claude apply it correctly.
// Prompt — works from memory, conversation, or samples
05 / audience.md
Who you serve
// ICP, pain points, offer, their language
Your People
audience
.md
Their pain, their language, your offer

This is everything Claude needs to write for your audience — not about your audience. Their fears. Their wins. The exact words they use to describe their problem. And your offer, explained in plain terms.

  • // Describe their daily reality. Not demographics — what a Tuesday looks like for them. What frustrations they carry. What they're quietly afraid of.
  • // Paste real language from real people. Comments, DMs, reviews. The exact phrases your audience uses are gold. Claude mirrors this language — and it's what makes content feel like it was written for them specifically.
  • // Name their dream outcome. Not what you think they want — what they actually say they want. Write it in their words, not yours.
  • // Include your offer here. What it is, why they buy it, who it's not for. Claude needs this to write CTAs, pitch copy, and anything that converts.
// Prompt — Claude interviews you, then researches your audience on Reddit and the web
