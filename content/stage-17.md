## 🧮 The PRICE Framework (Capstone Pricing Math)

How to price any of these builds.

- **P — Problem severity.** How much does this problem cost the buyer in time or money per month?
- **R — Repeatability.** Is this a one-time setup or recurring value? Recurring value commands recurring pricing.
- **I — Implementation effort.** What does it cost YOU to build and maintain?
- **C — Competition.** Are there direct competitors? What do they charge?
- **E — Evidence.** Do you have proof it works? Each build comes with a case study — use it.

**Rule of thumb:** charge 10% of the annual value the tool creates. A tool that saves a client 5 hours/week at $100/hour saves $26,000/year. Charge $2,600/year or $216/month.

## 📞 Database Reactivation (The Productized Play)

Every local business has a list of past customers they've never emailed again. That list is money sitting dormant.

**The build:** connect to the client's CRM or email list, segment customers by last purchase date, and generate a reactivation campaign — one email per segment, all in the client's voice.

**The result:** typically 5–15% of dormant customers respond to a well-crafted reactivation campaign. For a business with 500 past customers, that's 25–75 new bookings from one email.

**Productize it:** charge $500–1,500 for the setup + a percentage of recovered revenue. The client only pays when it works.

## 📊 The RICE AI Audit Framework (Animated Deck)

A productized audit you can sell to any business for $500–2K.

**RICE stands for:**
- **R — Revenue impact:** where is AI saving or generating money right now?
- **I — Implementation ease:** which AI tools can this business realistically adopt in 30 days?
- **C — Competitive gap:** what are competitors doing with AI that this business isn't?
- **E — Execution risk:** what could go wrong, and what's the mitigation?

**The deliverable:** a 10-slide animated deck (built with Claude Code + the 3D Websites kit) with the audit findings and a prioritized implementation roadmap.

**How to sell it:** offer the audit free or at cost. The implementation retainer is where you make money.
