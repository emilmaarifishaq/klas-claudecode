Then it builds a complete analytics dashboard that tracks subscribers, views, watch time, video velocity, and competitor gaps. All from a single prompt.

**Full YouTube Channel Analyzer Prompt** (requires vidIQ MCP):

```
YOUTUBE CHANNEL ANALYZER — FULL COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS
(Requires: vidiq MCP tools connected)

VARIABLES — fill these in before running:
MY_CHANNEL_HANDLE = "@YourHandle"
COMPETITORS = ["@Competitor1", "@Competitor2", "@Competitor3"]
ANALYSIS_WINDOW = 90   # days back for recent performance
BASELINE_WINDOW = 180  # days back for long-term baseline
THUMBNAIL_SAMPLE = 6   # recent videos per channel to pull thumbnails from

STEP 0 — SETUP
Call vidiq_user_channels to get your own channel ID and confirm the handle matches.
For each competitor handle, call vidiq_channel_stats to resolve their channel ID.

STEP 1 — DATA COLLECTION (run all calls in parallel)
For MY_CHANNEL_ID, pull:
  - vidiq_channel_stats
  - vidiq_channel_analytics (ANALYSIS_WINDOW days)
  - vidiq_channel_performance_trends (7d, 28d, 90d)
  - vidiq_channel_videos(type="short", sort="popular", limit=5)
  - vidiq_channel_videos(type="short", sort="recent", limit=5)
For EACH competitor, pull:
  - vidiq_channel_stats
  - vidiq_channel_performance_trends
  - vidiq_channel_videos(type="long", sort="popular", limit=8)
  - vidiq_channel_videos(type="long", sort="recent", limit=8)
  - vidiq_channel_videos(type="short", sort="popular", limit=5)

STEP 2 — COMPUTED METRICS
For MY channel, compute: daily view deltas, floor/average/spike days,
subscriber growth %, views-per-subscriber ratio, publish cadence,
performance distribution (median views at 7d/28d), outlier ratio.
For each competitor: growth %, views-per-subscriber, median 28d views, top 3 formats.

STEP 3 — OWN CHANNEL AUDIT
3a. Channel State: subscribers, 90d change, daily views, Shorts vs long-form split, cadence.
3b. Performance Distribution: median 28d views, outlier vs floor ratio, gap analysis.
3c. Content Pattern Audit: which topic clusters appear in top 10 vs floor performers.
3d. Title Pattern Audit: structural patterns in recent 10 titles, which work vs don't.

STEP 4 — COMPETITOR PROFILES
For each: stats, growth pattern, performance curve, content strategy, title patterns, key lesson.

STEP 5 — COMPARISON TABLES
Table 1: Growth (Subs, 90d New Subs, 90d Growth %, Median 28d Views, Views/Sub)
Table 2: Performance Curve (Floor, Median, Spike, Spike/Median Ratio)
Table 3: Content Format (% Shorts traffic, Primary long-form topics, Avg duration)

STEP 6 — CONTENT GAP ANALYSIS
Overcrowded topics / My unique lane / Competitor-exclusive / White space

STEP 7 — TITLE STRATEGY
Patterns to retire / 5 proven competitor formulas / 5 title rewrites (BEFORE → AFTER)

STEP 8 — THUMBNAIL ANALYSIS
Visual system, copy patterns, top performer patterns, cross-channel patterns, recommendations.

STEP 9 — 10 PRIORITIZED NEXT MOVES
List exactly 10 actions ordered by impact × feasibility.
Format: [N]. [ACTION] — [reason] — [timing: do now / next video / next month]

OUTPUT: 7 sections: My Channel Audit / Competitor Profiles / Comparison Tables /
Content Gap Analysis / Title Strategy + Rewrites / Thumbnail Analysis / 10 Next Moves
```

**Clone Yourself Prompt:**

```
I want you to create a comprehensive data packet of
everything you know about me in markdown format.
Include my name, businesses, projects, goals,
communication style, preferences, frameworks I use,
tools I prefer, audience demographics, content pillars,
pricing structures, team members, recurring themes in
our conversations, and any other relevant context
you've learned about me over time.
Format this as a structured document with clear sections
and headers that I can paste into another AI assistant
so it immediately understands who I am, how I work,
and what I'm building. Be thorough. Include everything.
```

**Tone-of-Voice Extraction Prompt:**

```
Your task is to extract, formalize, and systematize their tone of voice
into a clear brand document.

Produce tone-of-voice guidelines that:
- Look and feel like a professional internal brand document
- Are clear, grounded, and obvious
- Are usable by humans or AI systems
- Reflect how this person actually writes, not how they "should" write

Required Output Structure:

Tone-of-Voice Definition
A concise paragraph defining the overall voice and presence.

Core Voice Principles
List 5-8 principles. For each: Principle Name / Plain-language explanation /
Observable traits pulled directly from the content. No abstractions.

Emotional Register
What the writer prioritizes emotionally, how they relate to the reader,
what the reader is meant to feel. Be specific.

Language & Style Rules
Sentence length and structure / Vocabulary preferences /
Clarity vs. cleverness / Jargon use or avoidance /
Rhythm, pacing, and emphasis.

Structural Writing Patterns
How openings are handled / How points are built /
How emphasis is created / How conclusions or transitions typically work.

Perspective & Point of View
How authoritative vs. conversational the voice is /
How certainty or conviction is expressed /
Whether the voice teaches, observes, challenges, reassures, or directs.

What This Voice Avoids
Specific behaviors, tones, or styles clearly absent from the writing.

Usage Guidelines (For Humans & AI)
What to prioritize first / What to check before finalizing /
How to tell if the voice has drifted.

Rules: Do not praise the writer. Do not add external frameworks.
Do not invent traits not supported by the content.
When finished, output only the completed guideline document.
```
