# STAGE 21 — COACHING CALL

## 🎤 Live Coaching Call — Watch Real Builds Get Reviewed

This stage contains recordings of live group coaching calls where Duncan reviews members' builds, answers questions in real time, and works through problems together with the community.

**What coaching calls cover:**

- Live code reviews of member projects
- Prompting and workflow critique
- Business model and pricing advice for AI-built products
- Debugging sessions for stuck builds
- Q&A on anything from the course

**How to use these recordings:**

1. Find the call that covers a topic you're working on
2. Watch the relevant section — timestamps are in the video description
3. Apply the feedback to your own project even if it was directed at someone else
4. Post your build in the community for async feedback if you can't make a live call

---

## 📅 How To Join Live Coaching Calls

Live coaching calls are held regularly in the community. Check the **Events** tab in the Skool community for the next scheduled call.

**Before the call:**
- Have your project link ready to share
- Prepare a one-sentence description of what you built and what you're stuck on
- Come with a specific question — "I need general feedback" is harder to answer than "should I price this at $97 or $197 and why?"

**During the call:**
- Duncan takes builds in the order they're submitted in the call chat
- Screenshot errors or demo the live build — showing is faster than describing
- Take notes on feedback given to others — it almost always applies to you too

---

## 💬 Getting Async Feedback

Can't make a live call? Post in the community with:

```
Title: [Build Name] — Feedback Request
What I built: [one sentence]
Live URL: [link]
Specific question: [what you want feedback on]
Stack: [what it's built with]
```

Tag **Duncan** for prioritized review. Other members also give excellent feedback — the community is active and supportive.

---

## 🎯 What Makes A Great Coaching Call Submission

Submissions that get the most useful feedback:

**✅ "I built a review request automation for local businesses. It works but I don't know how to price it — should I charge $500 setup + $150/month or just $299/month flat?"**

This is specific, actionable, and has a real decision attached.

**❌ "I built something with Claude Code and want to know if it's good."**

Too vague. Duncan can't give specific feedback without knowing what the build is, who it's for, or what "good" means in this context.
