## 💬 Vague vs. Surgical: The One Skill That Decides Everything

![Vague vs. Surgical](/images/stage3-vague-vs-surgical.png)

> **You'll walk away with:** the difference between a prompt that gets you a $50 template and one that gets you a $20,000-looking site.

The single biggest thing separating people who get junk from people who get *stunning* results isn't the tool — it's **specificity**.

### Vague (what beginners type)

> "Build me a website for my coaching business."

You'll get a generic, templated thing.

### Surgical (what gets the $20k look)

> "Build a one-page site for a 1:1 productivity coach. Audience: overwhelmed founders. Tone: calm, premium, lots of whitespace. Hero: a result-focused headline + one 'Book a call' button. Then: 3 outcomes (not features), one testimonial, a simple FAQ, a closing CTA. Palette: deep navy + warm off-white, one amber accent. Type: a confident serif for headings, clean sans for body, generous line height."

Same effort to *think* it through. Wildly different output.

## 🧱 Building Your First Site

> **You'll walk away with:** a real, professionally-designed website running and previewing on your machine — built entirely by talking, in under an hour.

Open Claude Code in a new empty folder. Pick the prompt closest to you below, paste it, and watch it build with a live preview. Then run the loop: look at the preview, say what's wrong in plain words ("bigger hero", "swap section 2 and 3", "warmer colours"), repeat.

### Watch for

- If something looks off, describe the fix, don't try to "code" it. "The buttons feel cramped" works.
- Ask Claude to show you the preview any time: "open the live preview so I can see it."
- It's normal to go 5–10 rounds. That's not failure — that's the workflow.

**Sample prompt:**

> Build me a modern, professional one-page website for [YOUR BUSINESS / IDEA]. Include: a bold hero with a result-focused headline and one clear CTA button, a brief "what I do" section, 3 outcomes or benefits, one testimonial or proof point, and a simple contact/booking section. Use my design.md for colors and fonts. Make it look premium — lots of whitespace, real hierarchy, no clutter. Open a live preview so I can see it as you build.

## 🎨 Make It Look Designed, Not 'AI'

> **You'll walk away with:** one signature, hand-made touch that takes your site from "clean" to "a real studio made this" — without slowing it down.

Your build is already designed and fast. This is a light, optional **level-up**: add ONE distinctive touch *on top of the site you just built* — don't rebuild it, just refine.

**Prompt:**

> Now add a few distinctive, hand-made touches to the site you just built — don't rebuild it, just refine what's there. Make these specific changes: add a hand-drawn underline swash (an inline SVG squiggle in my accent colour) under the hero headline, switch to a bolder editorial title treatment (larger, tighter tracking), and add one static "by the numbers" band (3 figures that prove the result — e.g. "47 clients · 4.9★ · 100% remote"). Keep it fast — no animations, transforms only.

## 💸 Build It To Convert, Not Just Look Pretty

![Build It To Convert](/images/stage3-convert.png)

> **You'll walk away with:** a site that doesn't just look good — it moves visitors toward ONE action and comes alive.

A pretty site that just sits there does nothing. This pass turns the site you built into a high-converting, *alive*, polished version — without rebuilding it.

**Prompt:**

> Now turn the site you built into a high-converting, alive, polished version — refine what's there, don't rebuild it.
> HERO IMAGE: Add a real hero image beside the copy — source a relevant, license-free photo from Unsplash.
> STICKY BAR: Add a slim sticky "Book a free call" bar that appears after 3s scroll and stays visible.
> SOCIAL PROOF: Insert a "$20 off first session" nudge above the reviews section.
> GUARANTEE: Add a simple guarantee line ("No results in 30 days? Full refund.") near the CTA.
> MOTION: tasteful entrance animations (fade-up, 300ms, transforms/opacity only — no scroll-jank).

## 🎯 10 Proven Websites Prompts

**1. Three Layout Variants First**

> I want to build a website for [BUSINESS / PURPOSE]. Don't give me just one layout — build me 3 genuinely different variants (for example editorial, bold/dark, and minimalist). Let me click through all three and see them side by side before we pick a direction and dive into components. Use the front-end design skill.

**2. Always Invoke the Front-End Design Skill**

> Build me a modern, professional website for [BUSINESS]. Use the front-end design skill — I do not want generic AI output. I want something that looks like a real studio built it: deliberate type scale, cohesive palette, proper whitespace, real hierarchy.

**3. Conversion-First Structure**

> Build a one-page site for [OFFER]. Conversion structure: bold hero with one CTA → what it does → social proof → value proposition detail → ONE friction-reducing CTA. One message per section — if you ask someone to do more than one thing, they do zero.

## 🏆 Show It Off — Ship a Live Site

The fastest way to feel like a builder is to put something **live on the internet** and send the link.

### 1. Your One-Page Personal Site (~15 min)

> Build me a one-page personal website for [YOUR NAME]. Include a hero with my name + one-line bio, an 'about' section, and links to [LINKS]. Make it look designed, not generic — pick a bold modern style. Then deploy it to Vercel and give me the live URL.

### 2. A Site for a Local Business (~20 min)

> Build a modern website for a [TYPE OF BUSINESS] called [NAME]. Include hero, services, testimonials, and a contact section. Make it convert — clear calls to action. Deploy it live and give me the URL.

### 3. Clone a Site You Admire (~15 min)

> Here's a site I love the design of: [URL or screenshot]. Rebuild its visual style for my business: [YOUR BUSINESS]. Keep the design language, swap all the content for mine. Deploy it.
